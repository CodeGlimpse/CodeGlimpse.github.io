## How to play

Turn with the arrow keys, WASD, the direction buttons, or a swipe across the board. Each fruit earns 10 points and gradually increases the pace. Hitting a wall or your own body ends the round.

While the board has focus, Space pauses or resumes. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses it automatically. You cannot reverse direction directly.
