## How to play

Wait until the panel turns green and says NOW, then react as quickly as you can. Five valid reactions complete a round and reveal your average time. Tapping early is a false start and does not count toward the five attempts.

Tap the panel or press Space or Enter while it has focus. After a result appears, tap again to start the next attempt.

P or Escape pauses and resumes. Pausing cancels the unfinished wait; resuming starts a new signal while keeping completed results. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses automatically.
