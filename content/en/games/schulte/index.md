## How to play

Select numbers 1–25 in order on a 5×5 grid. Correct numbers are marked; a wrong selection adds a mistake without changing the next target. Finish to see this round's time and mistakes.

Use arrow keys to move focus and Enter or Space to select. P or Escape pauses or resumes while the board is focused. Clicking outside the game or switching windows also pauses. Paused time is excluded; restarting shuffles the numbers and clears this round.
