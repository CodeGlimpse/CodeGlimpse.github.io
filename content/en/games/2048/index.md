## How to play

Use the arrow keys, WASD, the direction buttons, or swipe across the board. Adjacent equal numbers merge in the direction you move. A new tile appears after each valid move.

Reach 2048 to win. The round ends when the board is full and no adjacent tiles can merge. Restart begins a new round, and refreshing the page resets the current one.
