## How to play

Two people take turns on the same page, with Black playing first. On the 15×15 board, connect at least five stones horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win. These are freestyle rules without forbidden moves; overlines also win. A full board without a winner is a draw.

Select an intersection, then choose Place stone. On a phone, use the direction buttons to refine the selection. On a computer, you can also double-click a position, or use arrow keys followed by Enter or Space. Occupied intersections cannot be used again.

The latest move and winning line are highlighted. P or Escape pauses or resumes while the board is focused. Clicking outside or switching windows pauses automatically. Restart clears the board for a new round.
