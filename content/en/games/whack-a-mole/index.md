## How to play

Each round lasts 45 seconds. A hit earns 10 points, and each mole can only score once. Empty holes do not cost points. Moles stay above ground for less time as the round progresses.

Tap a visible mole or press the number shown on its hole, 1–9, while the board has focus. You can also select a hole with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space.

P or Escape pauses and resumes; paused time does not count against your round. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses automatically.
