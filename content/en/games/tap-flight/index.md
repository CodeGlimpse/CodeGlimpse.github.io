## How to play

A tap gives the bird a lift; gravity brings it back down. Pass an obstacle to earn one point. Touching an obstacle, the ceiling, or the ground ends the flight.

Tap the board or the Flap button. With the board focused, Space, Enter, or Up also works. Your first tap starts the flight, and each new key press gives one lift.

P or Escape pauses and resumes while the board has focus. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses automatically.
