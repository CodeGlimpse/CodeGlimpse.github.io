## How to play

Watch the whole pattern before repeating it. Each completed round earns one point, and the next pattern adds another pad. A wrong pad ends the round; completing ten patterns wins the game.

Tap the four pads or press their number keys, 1–4, while the board has focus. Input begins after the demonstration. Colours, labels and numbers distinguish the pads.

P or Escape pauses and resumes. Resuming replays the current pattern from the beginning. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses automatically.
