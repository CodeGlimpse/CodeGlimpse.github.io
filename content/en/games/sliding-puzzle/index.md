## How to play

Arrange 1–8 from left to right, top to bottom, with the empty space in the bottom-right corner. Every starting position can be solved.

Tap a tile directly next to the empty space to slide it in. Arrow keys, WASD, the direction buttons, or a swipe move the empty space in that direction.

Space pauses and resumes while the board has focus. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses automatically.
