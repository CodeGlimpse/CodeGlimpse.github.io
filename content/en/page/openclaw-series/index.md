These three articles follow a local installation through its lifecycle. Read each article's review date and scope, then choose the steps for your system.

1. [Installation and configuration]({{< relref "post/openclaw-install" >}}): check Node.js and package-manager versions, run onboarding, and verify the Gateway.
2. [Browser connection modes]({{< relref "post/openclaw-chrome" >}}): distinguish the managed browser, Chrome DevTools MCP, and Chrome extension.
3. [Removal and cleanup]({{< relref "post/openclaw-uninstall" >}}): preview removal scopes and handle the service, state, workspace, and CLI separately.

Use the [JSON tool](/en/tools/json/) to practice with ordinary JSON examples, or [Text Diff](/en/tools/diff/) to compare two sample configurations. Full OpenClaw configurations may use JSON5; the JSON tool does not replace OpenClaw's own diagnostics.
