## How to play

Each brick earns 10 points. Clear all 28 bricks to win. You have three lives; after a missed ball, launch again when you are ready.

Move the mouse or drag across the board to steer the paddle. You can also hold Left/Right, A/D, or the buttons below. Tap the board, press Space, or select Launch to serve. Hitting near a paddle edge sends the ball out at a wider angle.

P or Escape pauses and resumes while the board has focus. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses automatically.
