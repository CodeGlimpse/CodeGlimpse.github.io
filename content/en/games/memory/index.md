## How to play

Turn over two cards at a time. Matching pictures stay face up; different pictures turn back over. Remember their positions and find all eight pairs.

Click or tap a card, or use Tab to select it and Enter or Space to flip it. Pausing covers the cards until you resume. Restart shuffles a new round.
