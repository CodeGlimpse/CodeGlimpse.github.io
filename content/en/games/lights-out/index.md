## How to play

Press a light to toggle it and its neighbours directly above, below, left and right. Turn off every light on the 5×5 board to win. Every starting board has a solution.

A filled circle means on; an empty circle means off. Tap a cell, or select it with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space. Try to solve the board in as few moves as you can.

P or Escape pauses and resumes while the board has focus. Clicking outside the game or switching windows pauses automatically.
